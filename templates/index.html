Standard Operating Procedures

Action plans and precautions for different disaster types.

Flood / Tsunami

  • Evacuate immediately to higher ground designated as safe zones.
  • Do not walk or drive through flood waters (6 inches of moving water can knock you down).
  • Turn off utilities at the main switches or valves if instructed.
  • Disconnect electrical appliances and do not touch electrical equipment if you are wet.
  • Listen to emergency broadcasts and follow instructions strictly.
  • Keep emergency kits ready and easily accessible.

Cyclone / Hurricane

  • Secure windows and doors; board them up if possible.
  • Stay indoors in a windowless room or hallway on the lowest floor.
  • Prepare for power outages by having flashlights and batteries ready.
  • Store adequate drinking water and non-perishable food.
  • Do not go out during the eye of the storm.
  • Secure loose objects outside that could become projectiles.

Earthquake

  • Drop, Cover, and Hold On. Stay away from glass, windows, outside doors.
  • If indoors, stay there. Do not run outside while the ground is shaking.
  • If outdoors, move away from buildings, streetlights, and utility wires.
  • If in a vehicle, stop as quickly as safety permits and stay in the vehicle.
  • Be prepared for aftershocks and check for structural damage.
  • Avoid using elevators during or after the quake.

Wildfire

  • Evacuate immediately if ordered by authorities.
  • Keep indoor air clean by closing all windows and doors.
  • Wear protective clothing (long sleeves, pants, heavy shoes) if going outside.
  • Have an emergency supply kit packed in your vehicle.
  • Back your car into the driveway or park in an open space facing the direction of escape.
  • Turn on lights outside to make the house more visible in heavy smoke.
